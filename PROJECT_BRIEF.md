# FlowCheck

FlowCheck helps housing investors find London boroughs that fit their available capital and risk appetite. Customers set a cash budget, select a historical price-variability band and choose a housing priority. An interactive map highlights matching areas, combining official house-price references with migration, housing delivery and vacancy evidence. Every match includes a transparent budget calculation, original sources and practical questions to investigate before committing capital.

## Hackathon submission abstract

London's housing debate combines the movement of people, investment and the delivery of homes, but those figures describe different things. FlowCheck turns that fragmented evidence into an interactive area-screening tool. Customers enter available capital, choose a risk appetite and prioritise population growth, housing delivery shortfalls or recorded empty homes. The map highlights matching boroughs and explains why each area fits or falls outside the brief.

The prototype joins all 33 London local authorities using official area codes. Capital references use archived March 2025 UK House Price Index averages plus editable works and reserve assumptions. Risk appetite filters relative historical price-index variability; it does not predict returns or losses. Migration, delivery and vacancy indicators remain separately dated, and foreign direct investment is shown at London level rather than allocated to boroughs without evidence. Customers can save areas, export a shortlist and generate source-based investigation briefs. The result supports the next property or policy investigation while keeping the limits of the evidence visible.

Customers can now click any London borough to open its district map, showing the official GLA 2025 electoral wards. The view includes 704 wards across all 33 boroughs, district names and area codes, keyboard selection, and a Back to London control. Capital and risk calculations continue to describe borough-level evidence.
